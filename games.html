    Halftime study break
    At the halfway point the game pauses and everyone reads the study sheet, on phones and the big screen, until you tap RESUME. At the end of the game students can save the sheet as a picture.
    1. Copy everything in this box. It already has your questions in it. 2. Paste it into an AI chat. 3. Replace everything here with the AI's answer. Words wrapped in **double stars** show up highlighted. The study sheet is saved with the quiz when you click Save to bank.
    Make the English study sheet first. Then click Copy translation prompt, paste it into an AI chat, and paste its Spanish study sheet here. It is saved with the quiz.
    Solo practice link (play at home)
    Makes a link with these questions, the study sheet and the game settings built in. Post it on Canvas or your class website and students can play on their own anytime, with no password. Solo scores stay on the student's device and are not sent to you.
