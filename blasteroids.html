HOW TO PLAY

⚔ COMBAT WAVE

  • Destroy all asteroids to clear the sector and advance
  • Large rocks split into mediums, mediums into smalls — smalls score most
  • UFOs appear in later waves — they fire at you, worth 500 pts
  • Surviving asteroids carry over into the next wave — stay alive

⚡ WARP CORE RETRIEVAL

  • Fly to each corner — a warp core sits inside a deflecting shield
  • Enter the inner ring to grab it — it follows behind your ship
  • Carry it to the glowing WARP NEXUS at screen center to deliver
  • Deliver all 4 cores for +1000 pts and wave completion

🪐 SOLAR DEFENSE

  • Protect all 8 orbiting planets from incoming asteroids
  • A warning names the targeted planet — intercept before it hits
  • The dashed threat line connects each asteroid to its target
  • Each planet has HP — losing one costs you a ship

👾 GALAGA GAUNTLET

  • Asteroids rain from above — move left/right and shoot them down
  • Rocks that pass the bottom wrap back to the top at full size
  • Medium rocks have a 20% chance to release a colored alien when destroyed
  • Red = fast dive-bomber · shoots aimed blasts · Green = zigzag · shoots aimed blasts
  • Blue = swooper · drops gravity bombs straight down · dodge by moving
  • Yellow = dive-bomber · fires a rainbow tractor beam · lethal at close range
  • All aliens die in one shot — but watch for their weapons!

🎮 CONTROLS

  • Joystick — push left half to turn left, right half to turn right
  • ▲ button — thrust / accelerate (Galaga: always-on engine)
  • 🔥 button — fire (tap rapidly for rapid fire)
  • Keyboard: Arrow keys or WASD + Space to fire
  • A 5-second shield is granted at every wave transition
  • Answer 3 questions right before every wave and to launch each new ship
